HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN FRIEND PHOTOS
===============================

1. Copy your funny photos into this folder:
   C:\Users\cemp\Desktop\rifa\assets\

   Example:
     assets/aarav.jpg
     assets/priya.jpg
     assets/rohan.png

2. Open script.js and find the FRIENDS array (at the top, clearly marked
   "EDIT YOUR PHOTOS HERE").

3. Replace the placeholder paths:
     photo: "assets/friend1.svg"
   with your own:
     photo: "assets/aarav.jpg"

4. You can also change each friend's name, title, and emoji fallback.

5. Recommended: square photos, 400x400px or bigger, JPG or PNG.
   Funny candid photos work best for the exhibition!

6. Keep the filenames simple (no spaces). Example: friend-aarav.jpg

7. Reload with Live Server - no build step needed.
